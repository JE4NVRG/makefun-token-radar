Pick a protocol to read its plan.

How the implied value is built

Every figure below comes from the sealed snapshot and nothing else. No name, no ranking and no outside price enters the number. 

implied = TVL × category ratio × growth adjustment × age adjustment

Category ratio

A prior FDV/TVL multiple for the line of business, stated as an assumption:

    Growth adjustment

    1 + 0.6 × clamp(7-day TVL change ÷ 100, −0.5, 1.2). A protocol that lost half its TVL in a week is discounted, not deleted; one that tripled is capped at 1.72×. A protocol whose seven-day change is unreported keeps 1.00×.

    Age adjustment

    0.85× on the day DefiLlama starts tracking a protocol, rising to 1.15× at five years. A protocol that has run this long with no token has already had every chance to launch, so its record is worth more than a month-old protocol's untested TVL.

    The implied value is this radar's own claim about a hypothetical token. It does not say a token will be issued, and it is not a price forecast. Protocols whose products wrap a token that already trades, and those that record one, are set aside rather than ranked.
